Which ETFs Hold Planet Labs ($PL) Stock? The Full List for 2026

The space funds with the biggest Planet Labs weights, the popular ETFs that don't own it, what the company actually does, and the SpaceX catch hiding in every one of them.

Next come $MARS (about 3.4%), $ORBX (about 3.3%) and $UFO (about 3.0%). $NASA holds a smaller 1.35%. The ARK funds, $ROKT and the big defense ETFs don't show it in their top 25 holdings. Weights are as of October 29 to November 2, 2026 and change daily.

My lists of ETFs that hold SpaceX, Rocket Lab, AST SpaceMobile and Firefly Aerospace keep getting found on Google, so I'm doing the next one people ask about: Planet Labs.

Planet is the company that photographs the entire Earth, basically every day, with a fleet of small satellites. It doesn't build rockets. It sells the data. That makes it a different kind of space stock than most of the names in these funds.

It's also been a wild ride. $PL closed at $13.47 on October 30, 2026. Its 52-week range is $10.52 to $51.76, and its market cap is about $4.9 billion. When a stock can swing that much, a lot of people would rather own it inside a basket. Here's every way to do that.

What You're Actually Buying With Planet Labs

Before I buy any fund for one name, I want to know what the name actually is. Quick version:

  • The business: Planet designs, builds and launches its own satellite constellations and sells the imagery and data through an online platform. Its SuperDove satellites aim to image the whole Earth every day at up to 3.5 meter resolution.
  • Newer satellites: its second generation Pelican high resolution satellites (Pelican-12 launched in October 2026) and Tanager, a hyperspectral satellite. In October 2026, Planet Labs Federal won a NASA evaluation task order for Tanager data under NASA's Commercial Satellite Data Acquisition program.
  • The numbers: about $378 million in revenue over the last 12 months, up 44%, but a net loss of about $360 million. Next earnings are expected December 9, 2026.
  • The basics: founded in 2010, based in San Francisco, listed on the NYSE under $PL.

So you've got real, growing revenue from a business that's more "data company" than "rocket company," but it's still losing a lot of money. That's a lot of risk to concentrate in one stock. A basket spreads it out.

Which Approach Fits You

  • "I want the most Planet Labs in a fund, for the lowest fee." $WARP, 4.01% at 0.50%. $ORBX is the runner-up at the same fee.
  • "I want a space fund with a longer track record." $UFO, launched in 2019, $PL at about 3%.
  • "I want a space fund big enough to stick around." $NASA, at about $984 million, but only 1.35% $PL.
  • "I own $ARKX, $ROKT, $ITA or $XAR." You don't have meaningful Planet Labs exposure. Just know it.

My Take

I'm a dividend investor first. I reached financial freedom by building income, not by betting on satellite stocks. Planet Labs doesn't pay a dividend, and a company losing hundreds of millions a year won't anytime soon.

So if I wanted exposure, I'd keep it small, and I'd rather get it through a basket than the single stock. Between the funds, I'd look at the fee first. $WARP and $ORBX hold as much or more Planet Labs than the 0.75% funds and charge 0.50%. That difference compounds every year you hold it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which ETF has the most Planet Labs stock?

Among diversified ETFs, the VanEck Space ETF ($WARP) had the biggest Planet Labs weight at about 4.01% as of October 30, 2026. Roundhill's $MARS (about 3.38%), Global X's $ORBX (about 3.31%) and Procure's $UFO (about 2.97%) were next. Tema's $NASA held about 1.35%.

Does $UFO hold Planet Labs?

Yes. Planet Labs was about 2.97% of the Procure Space ETF ($UFO) as of October 29, 2026, its 13th largest holding. SpaceX was its largest holding at about 14.86%.

Does $ARKX own Planet Labs?

Planet Labs did not appear among the top 25 holdings of the ARK Space & Defense Innovation ETF ($ARKX) in StockAnalysis.com data from October 29, 2026. $ARKX's largest holding was SpaceX at about 10.64%.

Is there a Planet Labs ETF?

I did not find an ETF that holds only Planet Labs shares. To own it inside a fund, space ETFs like $WARP, $MARS, $ORBX and $UFO each hold roughly 3% to 4% of their assets in $PL, alongside SpaceX, Rocket Lab and other space companies.

Is Planet Labs stock publicly traded?

Yes. Planet Labs PBC trades on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker $PL. It closed at $13.47 on October 30, 2026, with a market cap of about $4.9 billion and a 52-week range of $10.52 to $51.76.
